01 / THE OVERVIEW

A multidisciplinary
research experience.

During my internship at IIT Bhilai, I worked across two distinct technical tracks: understanding an existing FPGA-based CNN object-detection pipeline and preparing annotated lunar South Pole maps.

The work began with introductory Python learning, moved into QuickMap annotation and SystemVerilog architecture study, and concluded with detailed cartographic layouts in QGIS. This portfolio records the work completed, the tools explored, and the practical limitations encountered.

VIVADO · TROUBLESHOOTING

Project dependencies were missing

The Vivado Sources view reported that the referenced real_detect.bd block design and real_detect_wrapper.dcp checkpoint could not be found. Recovery attempts did not restore these files during the internship.

As a result, exact project restoration and gate-level mapping were not completed. No LUT, flip-flop, gate-count or timing-closure result is claimed.

OUTPUT-SIDE ESTIMATE

HDMI / RGB565 data-rate calculation

Values below reproduce the estimates recorded during the internship. They are arithmetic estimates, not independently measured HDMI output specifications.

QuantityRecorded estimate
Raw pixel payload1.188 Gbps (≈ 148.5 MB/s)
Pixel clock74.25 MHz
TMDS per data lane742.5 Mbps
Total across 3 lanes2.2275 Gbps

Engineering note: 1.188 Gbps equals 74.25 MHz × 16 bits/pixel, and 742.5 Mbps/lane equals 74.25 MHz × 10. A processing throughput of 6.67 FPS is not enough to derive the pixel clock without the active frame dimensions and video timing. Confirm resolution, blanking, color mapping and HDMI configuration before describing these as measured output rates.
